## Purpose

Project Atlas is Aster Vale Investments' compliance evidence modernization project. Its goal is to organize records showing that internal investment operations follow documented processes. Atlas covers evidence collection, review ownership, and audit preparation. It does not select investments or forecast fund performance.

## Ownership

Maya Chen is the project lead for Project Atlas. Daniel Ortiz is the executive sponsor. The project lead coordinates delivery; the executive sponsor resolves escalations. Being the sponsor does not by itself establish who approved a particular decision.

## Team and tools

Maya Chen and Priya Shah work on Project Atlas. The team uses the fictional Beacon Evidence Hub to organize compliance evidence and the fictional LedgerCheck tool to check evidence completeness.

## Decision records

Individual decisions and their approvers will be documented in separate decision records. This charter does not identify the approver of the Beacon Evidence Hub selection.

## Scope boundaries

This charter contains no expected investment returns, client account information, or portfolio holdings. The project is about organizational knowledge and compliance operations.
