## Daniel Ortiz

Daniel Ortiz is Chief Operating Officer in the Executive Office. He handles executive escalations for internal operations. An executive role or sponsorship does not establish approval of an individual project decision; consult the relevant decision record.

## Priya Shah

Priya Shah is a compliance operations analyst in the Compliance Operations team. Her responsibilities include assessing evidence workflows and coordinating evidence completeness reviews. Compliance Operations is a team within the Compliance Office.

## Elena Brooks

Elena Brooks is Head of Compliance and leads the Compliance Office. She reviews operational control proposals. Approval authority for a particular decision must be established from its decision record, not inferred from her title.

## Alex Morgan

Alex Morgan is a data engineer in the Data Platform team. Alex Morgan implements data-quality checks and maintains ingestion validation workflows. Alex Morgan is a different person from Alex Rivera.

## Alex Rivera

Alex Rivera is a risk analyst in the Risk Analytics team. Alex Rivera maps the lineage of internal risk reporting inputs and reviews reporting dependencies. Alex Rivera is a different person from Alex Morgan.

## Sofia Alvarez

Sofia Alvarez is the Risk Analytics team lead. She coordinates risk-reporting review work and maintains the team's operational reporting inventory.

## Name resolution

The first name Alex refers to more than one employee in this directory: Alex Morgan and Alex Rivera. If a request only says Alex and gives no distinguishing team, surname, project, or responsibility, ask which person is intended. A shared first name is not evidence that two records describe the same employee.

## Scope

This directory describes fictional organizational responsibilities. Skills are documented in the skills and tools register; project assignments and decision approvals are documented in their respective sources. It contains no client accounts, portfolio holdings, compensation data, or investment return promises.
