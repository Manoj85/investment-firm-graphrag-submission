## Purpose

Project Harbor standardizes access-request ownership and review records for internal operational platforms. Its aim is to make access reviews traceable, not to evaluate investments or predict returns.

## Ownership

Noah Singh is the project lead for Project Harbor. Daniel Ortiz is its executive sponsor. The project lead coordinates delivery; the sponsor handles executive escalations. Individual approval facts belong to decision records.

## Team and tools

Noah Singh, Alex Morgan, and Maya Chen work on Project Harbor. Harbor uses Harbor Access Register to record access-request ownership and review outcomes. Alex Morgan implements validation checks for the access-review records.

## Dependencies

Harbor provides its access-review records as an upstream input to Project Borealis. Harbor does not own Borealis's risk-reporting lineage inventory. DEC-002 documents approval of Harbor's access-review workflow; DEC-003 documents registration of Harbor Access Register as a Borealis source.

## Scope boundaries

This charter does not specify procurement prices, production launch dates, client accounts, or investment returns. A workflow approval is not evidence that deployment has completed.
