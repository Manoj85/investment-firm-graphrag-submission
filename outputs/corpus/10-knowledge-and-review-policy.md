## Purpose

This fictional operating policy describes how Aster Vale Investments records organizational roles, operational decisions, and supporting evidence. It applies to the internal projects Atlas, Harbor, and Borealis.

## Source authority

Use project charters for project leads, sponsors, and participants. Use individual decision records for the stated decision and its approver. Use the people directory for team membership and the skills and tools register for recorded expertise and tool responsibilities. A title or sponsorship is not a substitute for an explicit decision approval.

## Identity and ambiguity

People with the same first name remain separate identities unless their records establish they are the same person. A request mentioning only Alex without distinguishing context needs clarification between Alex Morgan and Alex Rivera. Missing identity context differs from a missing organizational fact.

## Evidence and corrections

Keep a document ID, section, version, and supporting excerpt with each recorded organizational relationship. A statement that one document omits a fact applies to that document; another authoritative source may establish it. If two authoritative records disagree about the same fact, record the conflict for owner review rather than silently choosing one. Source owners review corrections before publishing a new version.

## Scope boundaries

These sources describe fictional internal operations. They do not provide client records, portfolio holdings, personalized investment advice, expected annual investment returns, or return guarantees. Operational approval dates do not establish production deployment dates.
