## Decision and project

Decision DEC-001 selects Beacon Evidence Hub as the evidence organization tool for Project Atlas. The decision was approved on October 12, 2026. It concerns internal compliance operations, not investment selection or expected returns.

## Approval and accountability

Elena Brooks, Head of Compliance, approved DEC-001. Priya Shah prepared the evidence workflow assessment supporting the recommendation. Preparing an assessment is a different responsibility from approving the decision.

The Project Atlas charter records the project's lead and executive sponsor. Those project roles do not establish who approved DEC-001; this decision record is the authority for that approval.

## Reason for selection

Beacon Evidence Hub was selected to organize evidence records and their review ownership in one place. LedgerCheck remains the team's tool for checking evidence completeness. Selecting Beacon Evidence Hub does not replace LedgerCheck's completeness checks.

## Boundaries

DEC-001 approves the tool choice only. It does not record a deployment date, a procurement price, or an investment return guarantee. No deployment completion should be inferred from the approval date.
